Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glitchy Game Publishing collects and uses personal data when you visit this website or sign up for launch updates.

1. Who we are

Glitchy Game Publishing is a Belgian game publisher. For questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at glitchygamepublishing@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

When you sign up for updates, we collect the email address you provide. We do not ask for sensitive personal data through this website.

3. How we use your data

We use your email address only to send updates about the launch and related Glitchy Game Publishing news. We rely on your consent for this processing. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

4. Service providers

Signup responses are submitted through Google Forms. Google may process this information on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not sell your personal data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your email address only for as long as it is needed to send the updates you requested, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. We may retain a minimal record of your deletion request where necessary to respect it.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your personal data, as well as the right to withdraw consent. Email glitchygamepublishing@gmail.com to make a request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or services change. The latest version will always be published on this page with its updated date.
